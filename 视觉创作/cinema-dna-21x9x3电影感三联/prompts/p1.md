## Prompt（Shot 1 建立世界）

```text
Establishing shot of [SUBJECT: e.g. an old hotel in pink dusk ritual]. 24-28mm wide lens, medium-deep depth of field. Environment information exceeds character information. Subject occupies 5-15% of frame, placed in middle or third. Time of day: dusk. Weather: clear. Strong spatial order with one-point perspective. Character is distant, observing, approaching. Real film still, 35mm release print, soft gate, mild color breathing, fine natural grain. 21:9 aspect ratio.

aspect_ratio: 21:9
```
