# P3 · Anti-AI 电影画面纪律

## 5 字段

| 字段 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **核心主张** | 1 主要故事线索 + 1 次要线索 + 其余可无聊 |
| **认知锚点** | 禁用 8 词（rich detail / highly detailed / intricate / epic / dramatic / volumetric / beautiful / masterpiece）|
| **关键要点** | 每镜头 ≤ 2-3 场景细节 + 拒绝装饰性杂物/无处不在烟雾/均匀锐度 + 优先日常实用地点 |
| **数据** | 禁用 8 词 / 上限 2-3 场景细节 |
| **金句** | 镜头成立的原因 = 5 摄影参数 + 1 故事线索 + 0 装饰 |

## 配套 example

- `cabaret-aftermath-triptych.png` · Cabaret 主题 · 演出后空舞台感 · AI 套词难复刻

## Prompt（3 镜头 Anti-AI 包装）

```text
[SCENE TYPE]. 35mm film still texture, real narrative feature-film, not "cinematic" prompt. One primary story clue, one secondary clue, rest can be mundane, dark, soft, occluded, or empty. Cap at 2-3 concrete scene details plus camera position and light source. Reduce decorative debris, ubiquitous smoke, equally sharp details, shiny highlights, glossy wet surfaces, over-designed props. Ordinary practical location, human timing (waiting, missing, half-turning, leaving, hesitating, looking through glass, arriving too late). 21:9 aspect ratio.

aspect_ratio: 21:9
```

## 反模式

- ❌ 用 `rich detail` / `intricate` / `masterpiece`
- ❌ 每张 5+ 装饰细节
- ❌ 无处不在烟雾
- ❌ 完美镜面/湿润表面
